# StoryScroll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

StoryScroll is a personal project that automatically generates fictional story videos and publishes them to connected social media accounts.

## Information we use

When you authorize StoryScroll to connect to a social media platform, the platform may provide basic account information and authorization credentials required for the requested API features.

StoryScroll uses this information only to operate the authorized publishing features.

## Social media data

StoryScroll does not intentionally collect or sell personal information from users.

Authentication tokens and API credentials are stored locally by the project when required for continued authorization.

## Generated content

StoryScroll may generate fictional stories, narration, captions, and videos using third-party AI services.

## Third-party services

StoryScroll may interact with services such as TikTok, YouTube, Google APIs, and AI APIs to provide its functionality. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

## Data retention

Locally stored authentication information is retained only as long as needed for the project to operate and can be deleted by the project owner.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of StoryScroll changes.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the project owner through the associated social media or developer account.
